English
A lightweight iOS jailbreak tweak that brings the modern Home Bar (the little bottom swipe indicator) and an elevated Dock to older Home-button iPads — and iPhones too.

## 🌟 Features

- **Home Bar & Elevated Dock**: Faithfully recreates the full-screen Home Bar look and lifts the Dock up to match.
- **Works across devices**: Optimized for iPad, but runs on iPhone as well.
- **Two versions / modes**:
  - **Full / Modern Status Bar**: adds the Home Bar *and* replaces the top status bar with the larger full-screen style.（6）
  - **Lite / Native Status Bar**: adds only the Home Bar and elevated Dock, keeping the classic status bar untouched.（7）

## ⚠️ Known Issues

- **iPhone support**: the tweak runs on iPhones, but isn't a perfect fit. The flashlight and camera quick-access icons in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners may fall outside the visible screen area.




中文简体
一个轻量级的 iOS 越狱插件，旨在为配备 Home 键的旧款 iPad（及 iPhone）带来现代化的 Home Bar（小白条）体验与底栏抬高效果。

## 🌟 核心特性

- **小白条与底栏抬高**：完整还原全面屏设备的 Home Bar 视觉体验，并将 Dock 栏适度抬高。
- **跨设备支持**：主要针对 iPad 优化，同时兼容 iPhone。
- **双版本/模式选择**：
  - **完整版（Full / Modern Status Bar）**：在添加小白条的同时，将顶部状态栏替换为全面屏大号状态栏。（6）
  - **普通版（Lite / Native Status Bar）**：仅添加小白条与抬高底栏，保持原汁原味的经典状态栏。（7）

## ⚠️ 已知问题 / 注意事项 (Known Issues)

- **iPhone 适配**：本插件可在 iPhone 上运行，但非完美适配。在使用时，屏幕左下角与右下角的手电筒/相机快捷图标可能会超出实际显示区域。
